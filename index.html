Frequently asked questions

How do I compress an image online for free?
Drag and drop your image into ImageSqueeze, adjust the quality slider or choose a preset like "Smaller file" or "Tiny file," then click Download compressed image. It takes seconds.

Is it safe to compress private or sensitive photos here?
Yes. ImageSqueeze processes every image locally in your browser using HTML5 canvas — nothing is ever uploaded to a server, so your files stay fully private.

Which format should I choose — JPG, PNG, or WebP?
Use JPG or WebP to compress photographs and get the biggest file size reduction. Use PNG only for screenshots, logos, or graphics with text and sharp edges, since PNG is lossless and won't shrink photos much.
